# ADR-0115: live HDMI feed — Clef escalation retry, glyph-noise trimming, perceptual dedupe, per-source fallback threshold

Date: 2026-10-20
Status: accepted
Owners: live-hdmi-fixes worker
Related requirements/tests: docs/spec/05 (candidate retrieval → graph-aware scoring → optional Clef adjudication → commitment policy; "suppress duplicate OCR calls on unchanged region hash"), docs/spec/12 (frame replay, out-of-order results), docs/spec/13 (developer trace sample quotas), docs/spec/16 (M2 gates; bench holdout), ADR-0039 (fallback policy and defaults), ADR-0040 (VLM OCR fallback: the per-session engine, the rate limit, the crop cache), ADR-0050 (Clef adjudication in the pipeline), ADR-0055 (the live decision semantics this ADR extends: a Clef verdict selecting a retrieved candidate commits the line), ADR-0096 (OCR concurrency on the board), ADR-0100 (the warm OCR pool), ADR-0110 (the live view and the last-crop record), the 2026-10-20 live-session evidence (issue #24 findings)

## Context and constraints

A live session on the real UNO Q HDMI feed (640×480 v4l2 capture, real GameCube) produced the following evidence from the session events:

1. A real dialogue line was read correctly about 15 times in 50 s (Tesseract conf 0.88–0.95 on the crop), but its one ranking — `MATCH_FUZZY` at p=0.307 — was **never committed, never escalated to Clef and never re-ranked**: the rankings counter stayed at 3 while reads continued. The matcher's calibrated probability (T = 0.0728, ADR-0035 revision 2) put the fuzzy winner below any bar worth speaking, yet the ranking passed the matcher's own acceptance gates (which run on the revision-1 scale) and was therefore non-abstaining — so `NeedsAdjudication` (abstain, or margin below `AdjudicateMargin = 0`) never fired, and the pipeline's `head.ranked` key-dedupe marked the text "already answered", so no second ranking was ever dispatched.
2. Reads carry leading box-edge noise glyphs — "4 ", "i ", "5 ", "$o?" — the crop includes the textbox border and the speaker icon.
3. `ocr_skipped_by_hash = 0` over 335 frames while `ocr_stale = 194` and `ocr_deferred = 328`: the **exact**-hash skip never fires on analog HDMI noise, so every changed-pixel frame rides or restarts a read.
4. With the VLM fallback on, 14 of 15 reads fell back: the crop reads sit at 0.82–0.95 and the process-wide fallback threshold is a single constant (0.6) that is the wrong answer for both a noisy analog source and a clean emulator source.
5. The board is CPU-bound (narrator ~1.5 cores plus Tesseract); `dev_trace` encodes a full-frame JPEG plus one PNG per region **per changed-pixel frame** — on this feed, every frame — at capture rate.

Constraints: replay determinism and the bench holdout (recall ≥ 97.9 %, top-1 100 %) must not regress; every new skip path must stay honest in the counters (no "already read" claim about a read that was cancelled or never happened); rule 5 (no game text in the repo — all tests use invented lines); the per-region FIFO and the single-outstanding-ranking fence (the rankGate) are unchanged.

## Decision

### 1. Escalation retry: uncertain rankings are re-asked, bounded, and Clef's verdict commits

"Uncertain" is a named property of one ranking, owned by the session config so the policy and the evidence half agree (the same single-source rule ADR-0050 gave `NeedsAdjudication`):

- **`session.Config.AdjudicateFuzzyBelow`** (default 0.5; zero takes the default, negative disables): a non-abstaining ranking whose reason is `MATCH_FUZZY` and whose top candidate's *calibrated* probability is below the bar is uncertain. The calibrated probability is the scale the operator sees (the `p` in the evidence above); the matcher's gate scale deliberately stays untouched (ADR-0035 "Acceptance gate unchanged").
- `NeedsAdjudication` gains that clause. A locally-abstained ranking or a margin below `AdjudicateMargin` remains uncertain exactly as before, so the change is strictly widening.

The pipeline turns the property into a bounded retry:

- `pipeline.Config.EscalationRetryAfter` (default 10 s) and `EscalationMaxAttempts` (default 3, counting the first ranking of the occurrence): for one occurrence (box lifetime + normalized text), a ranking the policy calls uncertain does **not** settle the occurrence. On each later stable observation the pipeline re-dispatches the ranking once at least `EscalationRetryAfter` has passed since the previous attempt's reply landed and while fewer than `EscalationMaxAttempts` attempts have been made. A confident (non-uncertain) ranking settles the text as today (the `head.ranked` key-dedupe), and a new lifetime or a changed key resets the attempt count.
- The decision request carries the read text and the top **5** candidates (`pipeline.Config.DecisionCandidates`, default 5): the shortlist Clef is offered is the question, and five is the bounded options budget the request cap already implies (ADR-0050). The session's own `decide` still checks the chosen id against the full local ranking (a model may refuse but never supply).
- The commit is the ADR-0055 semantics: a Clef verdict selecting a retrieved candidate commits it as speakable (`CLEF_DISAMBIGUATED`, speech-eligible per the model's safe-to-speak); a fenced, refused or absent verdict leaves the local abstain as silence, and the retry budget is what says "ask again on the next stable read".

### 2. Glyph noise: trim non-word edge tokens before matching, inset the crop

- `matcher.TrimEdgeNoise` strips up to two leading and up to two trailing whitespace-separated tokens of at most two runes each (the box-edge glyphs "4", "i", "$o?" are exactly this shape), and only when a non-empty remainder survives. `Index.Rank` ranks the trimmed reading as well when it differs from the raw one and keeps the ranking whose top candidate's raw score is higher (ties keep the raw reading): the trim is evidence that may *help*, never a rewrite that may hurt, and a line that legitimately begins with a one-letter word ("I saw the boat") still wins on its exact raw match.
- `vision.CropOptions.Inset` (pixels, default 0) insets the mapped crop box on every side before cutting; the runtime sets 2 for its textbox regions so the decorative border and speaker icon stop entering the OCR input. The one-shot reader uses the same constant (its crops are the runtime's crops, ADR-0110's last-crop record draws them through the same options).

### 3. Perceptual dedupe: skip OCR when the crop is perceptually unchanged

- `vision.PerceptualHash` (a 64-bit dHash: the crop converted to 601 luma, downsampled to a 9×8 grid, one bit per horizontal neighbour pair) and `vision.PerceptualDistance` (Hamming) live in `internal/vision` beside `ImageHash`, stdlib-only, deterministic.
- The ROI stage keeps the dHash of the crop of the read it has outstanding or last ran (`regionState.lastPHash`, set when the read **starts** and cleared when it is cancelled or fails — the exact same discipline as the lossless `lastHash`, so "perceptually unchanged" is never claimed about a read that does not exist). A new crop within the tolerance (`pipeline.Config.PerceptualSkipTolerance`, default 10 of 64 bits) of that hash skips OCR: the observation is delivered with the last successful read's text (the tracker's reuse path), and the skip counts in `ocr_skipped_by_hash` — it is the same docs/spec/05 suppression with a tolerance, so the existing counter and OpenAPI surface are unchanged.
- The tolerance sits in the measured gap between a *new line* and its noise: analog HDMI noise shifts a stable box's 9×8 grid by single bits (the mean of ~25 source pixels per grid pixel absorbs the ±luma jitter), while a new line or a box change flips a majority of the glyph-bearing grid — well past 10. A new text *line* therefore always re-reads; only the noise rides.
- **The settle gate: quiet crops must run before the skip arms (issue #26).** Measurement on the replay corpus closed the remaining hole: a *one-word* typewriter growth moves 0–7 bits on the 9×8 grid — inside the tolerance. Tolerance alone cannot tell "noisy but still" from "one word just landed", and a skip that eats word-sized changes freezes a line at a prefix (the deterministic corpus caught it: the typewriter's two sub-tolerance steps were skipped and the box committed the stale prefix). So the skip requires a *run* of quiet crops first: `regionState.quietRun` counts consecutive crops within tolerance of the current anchor (identical-crop frames included) and only past `pipeline.Config.PerceptualSkipSettle` (default 3, clamped to 16) does the crop actually skip; a quiet crop inside a run of changes rides the in-flight read when one is outstanding (its pixels are already being paid for) or starts a read when none is — and any change beyond tolerance, new read, failed read, or box dismissal resets the run, because the settle is about the *current* anchor. A line that is still typing keeps producing changes beyond any 3-crop quiet run, so it is never skipped mid-growth; a stable line pays at most `settle` extra reads after it settles and then rides the last read for the rest of the page, which is where the live feed's per-frame OCR cost actually lives.

### 4. Fallback threshold: per source, env, profile

- **`GAME_NARRATOR_OCR_FALLBACK_CONFIDENCE`** (float in (0,1]): the process default for the VLM fallback's `ConfidenceThreshold`, read by `cmd/game-narrator`'s fallback wiring.
- When the process default is unset, the runtime fills a **per-source** default: v4l2 (the HDMI/analog capture) sessions take 0.85 — the measured floor of correct crop reads on this feed (0.82–0.95), so a correct read no longer spends a VLM call — while every other source kind keeps the ocr package's 0.6 default (a clean emulator crop at 0.7 is still worth a second opinion).
- A region's `ocr_settings` JSON may carry `{"fallback_confidence_threshold": <float>}` (the same home as ADR-0040's `difficult_font`): when any region sets it, the value wins for the session. Precedence: profile value > process env > per-source default.

### 5. dev_trace sampling: at most one sample per second of source time

`devTraceRecorder.recordFrame` gates on the frame's **source clock** (its `CaptureTimestampNS`; the wall clock when the frame names none): at most one encoded sample per second of captured time, whether or not the picture changed. The old gate skipped only an *unchanged* crop within 250 ms of wall time — on a noisy analog feed, where the crop hash changes every frame, it re-encoded the full-frame JPEG plus one PNG per region on **every frame**. The encode, not the store, is the per-frame CPU cost, measured on the development host: a 1280×720 full-frame JPEG at the dev-trace width plus one region-crop PNG costs ≈ 28.6 ms of encode (7-rep mean), so the old gate spent ≈ 860 ms/s — nearly a whole core — on dev-trace evidence alone at a 30 fps capture, the CPU the board could not spare; the new gate spends the same 28.6 ms once per second of source time (≈ 3 % of a core), a 30× reduction at 30 fps and the same factor at any capture rate. The unchanged-pictures-within-a-second case is unchanged in meaning: a stable picture still costs one encode per second at most, a page turn still gets its sample within a second. The interval is only compared within one clock domain (a frame named on a different clock than the last sample is stored: the fail-open direction, bounded by the byte quota). The quota, the lookup and the privacy story (developer role only, hashes on the wire) are untouched.

## Tradeoffs considered

- **Re-ranking the same key forever:** unbounded re-asks would turn a genuinely unreadable line into a Clef call every few seconds for the rest of the box's life; the attempt bound (3) and the 10 s cadence are the docs/spec/06 budget applied to escalation. The defaults are named constants, not magic numbers, and the config fields let a board-tuned value replace them without code.
- **Trusting the fuzzy commit instead:** lowering the matcher's `MinProbability`/`MinMargin` to force the fuzzy line to abstain would also abstain every legitimate fuzzy read in every corpus (the acceptance gate is shared; ADR-0035 pins its scale), and the session would still commit the abstain as silence — the retry is the mechanism the "same text keeps being observed" evidence asks for, and it costs nothing when the first attempt is confident.
- **Trimming only (no crop inset) or inset only (no trim):** the inset prevents border glyphs at the source but cannot remove glyphs the border already puts into the profile-calibrated rect (the speaker icon is *inside* the rect on this feed); the trim cleans what the inset lets through. Both are needed because neither is sufficient alone, and each is inert when the other's noise is absent.
- **A new `ocr_skipped_by_phash` counter:** a distinct counter would need an OpenAPI schema change and a developer-UI field for what is operationally the same fact ("OCR skipped because the crop is unchanged"); the existing `ocr_skipped_by_hash` partition stays meaningful (calls < frames is still explained), so the skip books there. Revisit with the evidence if the two skip classes ever need separate operators.
- **dHash vs aHash:** dHash's adjacent-pixel comparison is the better noise-tolerance/line-change contrast at 64 bits (aHash's 8×8 mean-difference saturates on a dark textbox where every pixel is near the mean); the tolerance, not the hash choice, is the knob the feed's noise sets.
- **A 4 Hz dev_trace gate (the old 250 ms) on the source clock:** 4 samples/s still spends 4–12 encodes/s of a core the board cannot spare, for evidence a human reads at page-turn rate; one per second is the docs/spec/13 "sample mode" quota made a rate rather than a byte count.

## Validation

- New unit tests: the perceptual hash (noise-stable within the tolerance, a changed line far from it), the crop inset, the edge-noise trim (raw wins when the raw is the exact line, the trim wins when the raw is border noise), the Rank-with-trim pick, the `NeedsAdjudication` fuzzy clause, the escalation bounds (cadence, attempt cap, key/lifetime reset), the per-source fallback threshold (profile > env > source default), the dev_trace one-per-second gate.
- The live-hdmi scenario tests (`internal/pipeline`, ModeLive, scripted OCR + scripted decision provider, a near-twin corpus in the live line's shape): (a) a stable line whose stable read ranks MATCH_FUZZY below the calibrated commit bar is offered to Clef with the read text and the top-5 shortlist, and a verdict that selects the retrieved runner-up commits it as speakable (`message.committed` reason `CLEF_DISAMBIGUATED`, the local top-1 is *not* what gets said); (b) the same line with Clef abstaining twice re-ranks on the retry's cadence until the bounded attempts run out — exactly `EscalationMaxAttempts` rankings, no fourth ask while the box stays READY — and the attempt the budget finally earns commits on the resume.
- The perceptual settle gate is covered by the pipeline's existing deterministic corpus tests (the typewriter's sub-tolerance steps must reach OCR — they regressed and were caught when the first cut of the skip ate them) and by `TestPhashToleranceSitsInTheMeasuredGap`, which pins the measured gap the tolerance and settle are tuned against: holds at 0 bits, word growths at 0–7 bits (inside the tolerance, the settle's reason to exist), the page change far past it.
- Regressions: `go test -race` on the touched packages, `make test-replay` (replay determinism, the replay gate), the bench (`bench --set testdata/bench/bench-synthetic-001 --split holdout`: recall ≥ 97.9 %, top-1 100 %).
- Board acceptance (separate, hardware gate): a live HDMI session with the VLM fallback on reports `ocr_skipped_by_hash > 0` on a stable box, no fallback calls on correct crop reads (the `ocr.fallback` trace rows say `primary_confident`), and a fuzzy line commits via `decision.resolved` within the retry budget.

## Consequences and reversal

- New: `internal/vision/phash.go` (+ tests), `CropOptions.Inset`, `matcher.TrimEdgeNoise` and the Rank-with-trim pass, `pipeline.Config`'s `EscalationRetryAfter` / `EscalationMaxAttempts` / `PerceptualSkipTolerance` / `PerceptualSkipSettle` / `DecisionCandidates`, `session.Config.AdjudicateFuzzyBelow` and the `NeedsAdjudication` clause, the per-source fallback threshold (runtime + `cmd/game-narrator` env), the source-clock dev_trace gate.
- Reversal: the new config fields are additive (zero/defaults restore the prior behaviour — the fuzzy clause disabled by a negative `AdjudicateFuzzyBelow`, the retry by `EscalationMaxAttempts = 1`, the perceptual skip by a tolerance of 0 or the settle by an arbitrarily long quiet-run demand, the inset by 0, the threshold by leaving the env unset); `phash.go` and the inset delete cleanly; the dev_trace gate reverts to the wall-clock 250 ms rule by restoring the one line in `recordFrame`. Nothing durable changes shape: no schema migration, no event-name change, the OpenAPI surface gains nothing.

## Amendment, 2026-10-23 — §3: a settled box whose newest pixels were never read gets exactly one confirming read

### Evidence

A local Dolphin run (2026-10-23, `TestLiveCatchUpRapidDialogue`) ended with a fully typed box on screen that the session emitted nothing for 45 s. The last `text.observed` read the typewriter prefix (the final characters missing); Tesseract read the final crop correctly when given it, but the final pixels never reached the engine. The mechanism, in §3's own machinery: the final crop differed from the prefix crop by a couple of characters — inside `PerceptualSkipTolerance`, so it took the perceptual-skip case; while the prefix read was still in flight the crop RODE that read (`p.job = rs.inflight`) and got the prefix's text, and once the settle armed (or was already armed) the perceptually-quiet repeats skipped on the anchor's text — pixels nobody had read. Every later frame was pixel-identical to the final crop, but the exact-hash dedupe never matched it (the anchor was the prefix), so the region never re-read and reused the prefix forever. §3's rule as written — "the perceptual memory is the same claim on a tolerant key" — let a crop whose *exact* hash was never read inherit the "already read" claim of pixels it is only perceptually close to.

### Decision

The dedupe key must describe the pixels that were actually READ. The fix is bounded — **at most one extra read per settled box** — because on the UNO Q's analog feed no two crops are ever pixel-identical (the `PerceptualSkipTolerance` comment in config.go), so "the settle armed on pixels that were never read" is true there on *every* settle period; re-confirming each time would put the static box back on the engine's cadence, which is the per-frame cost §3 exists to remove (the board is CPU-bound already).

A first cut of the fix — `owedRead`: any crop within tolerance whose exact hash differed from the anchor read's hash owed a read, `quietRun` reset to 0 on every such crop, and the crop read as soon as nothing was in flight — was **rejected**. It broke the analog feed by construction: every analog crop carries a distinct exact hash, so the debt was always set, the quiet run was reset on every frame, the settle never armed, and the region re-read at the engine's cadence forever. Measured on the synthetic analog clip below, that version settles **15 OCR calls in 60 frames with a skip counter of 0** (45 rides); the main code settles 1.

The final design:

- The quiet-run counting is unchanged from §3: `quietRun` runs on every crop within tolerance of the anchor (identical-crop frames included) and resets with the anchor (new read, failed read, cancelled read, box dismissal, change beyond tolerance).
- The perceptual-skip case only runs when the crop's exact hash is NOT the anchor read's hash (an identical crop is the exact-hash case, which skips exactly as before — a settle that arms on the read's own pixels is untouched). There, at the moment the settle ARMS (the first crop with `quietRun > PerceptualSkipSettle`):
  - if a read is in flight, the crop rides it (`ocrDeferred`) and the next quiet crop after it settles does the confirming read;
  - else, if the anchor is not yet confirmed, the region starts **one confirming read** of this crop. `startJob` re-anchors `lastHash`/`lastPHash` on the pixels actually read and resets the quiet run, so the skip re-arms on the new anchor;
  - else — the anchor was confirmed already — the crop skips on the anchor's text exactly as §3 measured.
- "Confirmed" is `regionState.confirmed`: set only by a confirming read, cleared by every other read (`startJob` takes the flag), and cleared with the rest of the anchor memory wherever the perceptual memory goes. It says "the last read confirmed a perceptually-unchanged box", so a re-armed settle on further unread pixels does not start a second confirming read.
- Net effect: Dolphin (identical repeats of a never-read final crop) gets exactly one read of the final pixels; the analog feed gets one extra read per settled box, then skips as today.

### Validation

- `TestRiddenFinalCropGetsExactlyOneRead` and `TestIdenticalCropsAfterRiddenReadSkip` (`internal/pipeline/ride_final_crop_test.go`, synthetic live clip: prefix crop, 300 ms read, final crop inside the 10-bit tolerance with a distinct exact hash, repeated identically): the final pixels are recognised exactly once, the region's emitted text ends as the final line, and the identical crops after the final read skip (no OCR storm).
- `TestAnalogNoiseSettleCostsOneConfirmingRead` (`internal/pipeline/analog_noise_settle_test.go`): a static box for 60 frames whose every crop has a distinct exact hash yet sits inside the perceptual tolerance of the anchor (the band walks a few pixels on coprime periods — the analog feed's distinct-hash property in crop form), 300 ms engine at 100 ms frames. Before (main code): **1 settled OCR call, 56 skipped, 3 deferred**. The rejected always-owe cut: **15 settled OCR calls, 0 skipped** — the per-frame cost §3 removed, back on the board. With this amendment: **2 settled OCR calls (the first read, exactly one confirming read), 52 skipped, 6 deferred**; the test asserts settled calls stay bounded at ≤ 3 and the skip counter covers the rest.
- Regressions: `go test -race -count=1 ./internal/pipeline/` clean; the replay gate (`make test-replay`) stays green — every historic replay test still passes, plus the three new tests; lint clean.

### Consequences and reversal

- The amendment is one flag (`confirmed`) and its bookkeeping in `internal/pipeline/dispatch.go`, plus the armed-settle branch of the perceptual case; the OpenAPI surface, counters, config and events are unchanged (the confirming read counts in `ocr_calls`, its rides in `ocr_deferred`, the skips in `ocr_skipped_by_hash`).
- Reversal: delete the `confirmed` flag and its set/clear sites, restore the armed branch to the plain `phashSkip`; the §3 settle-gated skip returns to its prior semantics (the frozen-final-crop case above comes back).
